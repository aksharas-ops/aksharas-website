# V41 – Approved homepage prototype alignment

This update corrects the desktop homepage hero to more closely match the supplied approved prototype.

- Restores the wide three-line hero headline by increasing usable text width and correcting typography scale.
- Aligns the left text panel and curved food slideshow to the prototype proportions.
- Removes the dense repeating kolam pattern from the hero background while retaining the hanging brass lamp, upper-left leaf accents, lower-left temple line-art and green ground flourishes.
- Retains the warm ivory background, orange curved image edge, three-image slideshow, CTA buttons and green benefits strip.
- Slows slideshow rotation to 9 seconds so the approved first South Indian food image remains visible longer.
- Keeps tablet and mobile layouts responsive.

No other website sections, forms, links, or business functionality are intentionally changed.
