# Aksharas Website V12 – Our Story Full Background

This update implements the approved Our Story prototype directly in the website:
- Removes the rectangular content panel, border, radius and shadow completely.
- Places the existing Our Story text directly on a full-width warm cream illustrated background.
- Adds subtle leaf accents, flowing green waves and a South Indian temple/banana-leaf food illustration.
- Keeps the heading and text as normal HTML, not flattened into an image.
- Includes responsive spacing and typography for mobile and tablet.

All previous V11/V10 website content and branch contact changes are retained.
